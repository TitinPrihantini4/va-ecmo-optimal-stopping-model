Recovery-drive decay
G(t) = G_floor + (G_0 − G_floor) exp(−k_G t)

Latent physiological reserve
dR/dt = k_R G(t)(1−R) − k_DR D R − k_AR A R

Oxygen-debt dynamics
dD/dt = k_D(1−R)(1−D) − k_clear R D

ECMO support-dependence dynamics
dA/dt = k_A S(t)(1−R)(1−A) − k_release R A

Hemodynamic-stability dynamics
dH/dt = k_H R(1−H) − k_HD D H − k_HA A H

LVOT-VTI observation equation
Y_VTI(t) = β_0 + β_R R + β_H H − β_A A + ε_VTI

Nonlinear liberation-feasibility mapping
π_L(t) = logistic(α_0 + α_V Y_VTI + α_R R + α_H H − α_D D − α_A A)

Failure-hazard propagation
λ_F(t) = λ_0 exp(γ_D D + γ_A A − γ_R R − γ_H H)

Dynamic liberation margin
Ψ(t) = w_R R + w_H H − w_D D − w_A A + w_V(Y_VTI−9.5) − b_0

Immediate-liberation utility
U_L(t) = a_S π_L − a_F(1−π_L) − a_D D − a_A A + a_H H − c_L

Continuation value
U_C(t) = exp(−ρΔt)V(t+Δt) − [c_0 + c_D D + c_A A + c_λ λ_F]Δt

Bellman recursion
V(t) = max{U_L(t), U_C(t)}

Optimal stopping time
τ* = inf{t : U_L(t) ≥ U_C(t), Ψ(t) ≥ 0, Y_VTI(t) ≥ 9.5, H(t) ≥ 0.55}

Latent clinical regime
Z(t) ∈ {Support-dependent, Recovering, Liberation-ready, Relapse-risk}

Continuous-time regime transition
Pr[Z(t+Δt)=j | Z(t)=i] = [exp{Q(X_t)Δt}]_ij

Jacobian matrix
J_ij(X*) = ∂f_i/∂X_j |_(X=X*)

Local stability criterion
max Re{λ(J(X*))} < 0

Partial rank correlation
PRCC(θ_j, Ψ_T) = Corr(rank-residual θ_j, rank-residual Ψ_T)

Akaike information criterion
AIC = 2k − 2 log(L)

Bayesian information criterion
BIC = k log(n) − 2 log(L)
